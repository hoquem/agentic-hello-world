# Title + thumbnail brief

## Title options (A/B test these)
1. **Hello World — but with an LLM** (clean, matches the hook)
2. **Your first "AI program" isn't what you think**
3. **Why an LLM can't reliably say "Hello World"**
4. **I made an LLM say Hello World. It got weird.**

Recommendation: **#1** as the title; keep #3/#4 energy for the thumbnail text.

## Thumbnail concept (1280×720, readable at tiny sizes)
- **Left half:** a code editor line — `print("Hello, World!")` — with a small green ✓ and the word **"ALWAYS"**.
- **Right half:** a chat bubble from a 🦙 with a glitch/multiple-overlapping-answers look — `"Hello World!"` / `"Hi there!"` / `"Ahoy!"` — and the word **"…MAYBE?"** in a warning color.
- **Big top text (3–4 words max):** `SAME CODE. / DIFFERENT ANSWER.`
- High contrast, dark background (matches the app), 1–2 colors of text. No clutter. Your face optional in a corner if you do talking-head.

## Notes
- The single curiosity gap is *determinism vs. not* — make the thumbnail say that in one glance.
- Keep text huge; assume it's seen at 10% size in a feed.
